Campus chaplaincy · Russell Sage College

Care for the whole student.

College is hard — and not just academically. TAUM's chaplaincy at Russell Sage College offers students and staff of every background (and every belief, or none) a caring presence on campus: someone to talk to, de-stress meditation sessions, care packages during finals, and space to figure out what you believe and who you're becoming.

We work alongside Sage's wellness and academic teams — chaplaincy here is about care, never conversion.
