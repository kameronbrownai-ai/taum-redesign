The Damien Center

Nobody should face a hard diagnosis alone.

The Troy Damien Center is a hospitality center for neighbors living with serious illness. What started in the 1980s as a refuge for people affected by HIV/AIDS, at a time when few doors were open to them, is today a warm community for anyone navigating serious illness: a place for meals, friendship, peer support, and help finding the services you need.

What you'll find here

🍽️ A seat at the table

A hot lunch and good company, weekdays 11:30 am to 1:00 pm, open to anyone experiencing food insecurity, too.

🎲 Joy is part of health

Social events, games, and recreation with people who get it.

💬 Peer support

Support from people who've been where you are, no lectures, no judgment.

🧭 Help navigating

Connections to medical, housing, and benefit services when the system feels impossible.

Our history matters to us

The Damien Center's roots in the HIV/AIDS crisis taught us everything about welcome without judgment. That welcome now extends to every neighbor facing illness.
