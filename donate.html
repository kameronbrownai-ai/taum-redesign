Every gift stays in the neighborhood.

When you give to TAUM, you're not funding overhead in some faraway office. You're stocking a fridge on Second Street, fueling a furniture truck, paying a teen's summer stipend, and setting a lunch table for a neighbor. Thank you.

Give money

  • Online: one-time or monthly, by card or PayPal
  • By mail: checks payable to Troy Area United Ministries, 392 2nd Street, Troy, NY 12180
  • Current campaign: Growing and serving, the 2026 garden expansion

Give things

  • Furniture: beds, dressers, tables, chairs, living room pieces, we pick up
  • Food: unexpired, labeled food for the pantry and Free Food Fridge, anytime

Give for the long haul

Remember TAUM in your will, or give to our Legacy Fund, established on our 30th anniversary. For stock gifts, planned giving, or foundation grants, talk with our Executive Director: nortonlevering@taum.org, (518) 274-5920 x202.

Give your time

Some of our most valuable gifts arrive wearing work gloves. Volunteer with us.

Where it lands

Your gift doesn't disappear into overhead. It becomes a box of groceries handed to a neighbor, a bed carried up three flights, a teen's first paycheck, help that stays right here in Troy.

TAUM is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit; gifts are tax-deductible. We'll send a receipt for every donation.
