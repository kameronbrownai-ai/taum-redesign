Food

Free food, no questions asked.

Nobody in Troy should go hungry. Whether you need a hot meal, groceries to cook at home, or something tonight when everything else is closed, come by. You don't need a referral, an ID, or a reason.

🍲 Hot meals

Join us for lunch in the Damien Center dining room, Monday to Friday, 11:30 am to 1:00 pm. Everyone is welcome at the table. Meals are cooked in our kitchen with help from volunteers and, in season, vegetables from our own garden.

🛒 Groceries

Need food for the week? Our pantry shelves are open during office hours, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 4 pm. Take what your household needs.

🥬 The Free Food Fridge

Outside our building at 392 Second Street, the Free Food Fridge is open 24 hours a day, every day. Take what you need, leave what you can. Neighbors and local partners keep it stocked with fresh produce, dairy, and prepared meals.

A place at the table

Food is about more than calories, it's company. Our dining room fills with neighbors from every walk of life, and there's always a seat for one more.

Want to help keep the fridge full?

Drop off unexpired, labeled food anytime, or make a gift and we'll do the shopping.
