Food

Free food, no questions asked.

Nobody in Troy should go hungry. Whether you need a hot meal, groceries to cook at home, or something tonight when everything else is closed, come by. You don't need a referral, an ID, or a reason.

🍲 Community meals

Two hot meals every week, cooked in our kitchen and open to everyone:

  • Monday evenings at 5:45
  • Thursdays at noon

We also host special meals through the year. Watch News and events for those.

🛒 Groceries

Groceries are distributed Thursdays at noon, alongside the Thursday meal. Come by and take what your household needs. No ID and no referral.

🥬 The Free Food Fridge

Outside our building at 392 Second Street, the Free Food Fridge is open 24 hours a day, every day. Take what you need, leave what you can. On days we are not serving a meal we try to keep prepared food in the fridge, along with fresh produce and dairy from neighbors and local partners.

When to come

Monday 5:45 pm for a hot meal. Thursday noon for a hot meal and groceries. Any other time for the Free Food Fridge, which never closes.

A place at the table

Food is about more than calories, it's company. Our dining room fills with neighbors from every walk of life, and there's always a seat for one more.

Our garden

We grow food right here. In season, vegetables from the TAUM garden go straight into the meals we serve and into the Free Food Fridge. Thanks to partners at Mohawk Global and Capital Roots, and gifts from the First Reformed Church of Schenectady and the Common Roots Foundation, we are expanding the garden in 2026.
