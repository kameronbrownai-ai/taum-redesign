The Furniture Program

A fresh start deserves a furnished home.

Since 1986, TAUM's Furniture Program has given good, used furniture, free of charge, to neighbors starting over: families leaving shelters, women and children escaping domestic violence, people recovering from a fire, seniors and neighbors living on very little. A bed to sleep in and a table to eat at shouldn't be luxuries.

Need furniture?

We work through referrals from local agencies, shelters, and congregations. If you're working with a caseworker, ask them to contact us. If you're not, call us directly and we'll help you figure out the next step. Furniture and delivery are always free.

Call Tyara Burnett, Program Director:
(518) 274-5920 x204

Have furniture to give?

We come to you. Call or request a pickup below and we'll schedule a free pickup from your home.

Keep the truck rolling

Every delivery costs money: gas, insurance, and the strong backs of our small crew. A gift of any size keeps furniture moving to the families who need it.
