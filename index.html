Troy Area United Ministries · Est. 1986

A neighborhood center where everyone belongs.

At 392 Second Street, TAUM opens its doors to the whole community — with free meals, groceries, and a Free Food Fridge; furniture for families making a fresh start; summer jobs and tech skills for teens; and care for anyone facing illness, homelessness, or hard times. Rooted in Troy's interfaith community, open to all.

What's growing at TAUM

Growing and serving: help our garden grow

With partners at Mohawk Global and Capital Roots — and generous gifts from the First Reformed Church of Schenectady and the Common Roots Foundation — we're expanding our community garden in 2026. Fresh vegetables grown here go straight to our meal program and Free Food Fridge.

2026 · Our 40th year

Forty years. Forty flames. One neighborhood.

TAUM turns 40 this year, and we're celebrating all year long. We're looking for forty friends — people, families, congregations, businesses — to each "light a candle" by hosting something for the community: a dinner party, a movie night, a garden build, an ice cream social, a fresh coat of paint on a wall that needs it.
