# TAUM Mission Statement — Draft Options
*Drafted 2026-07-19 from Abby's revision notes. For board discussion — not final.*

## Current (outgoing) statement
> "In partnership with interfaith communities and others, Troy Area United Ministries provides a safe and just space for those in need, offering meals, services for those affected by HIV/AIDS and other serious illnesses, furniture, education, spiritual counseling and advocacy."

## Revision requirements (from Abby)
1. Highlight food: meals, groceries, AND the Free Food Fridge
2. Highlight furniture
3. Keep interfaith roots without excluding non-religious people
4. Drop HIV/AIDS; broaden to illness + life challenges (esp. homelessness, domestic violence)
5. "Education" undersells Tech for Teens (jobs + tech skills)
6. "Spiritual counseling" misses the Sage chaplaincy and what happens at 392 2nd St
7. Add community/neighborhood center identity

## Key move: faith as origin, not audience
Put faith in *where we came from* ("founded by congregations across the Troy area") rather than *who it's for* ("open to people of every faith and no faith at all"). Honors history, excludes no one.

---

## Option A — short and warm (recommended)
> Troy Area United Ministries is a neighborhood center where everyone belongs. We share food and furniture, support neighbors through illness and hard times, and bring people together to build a stronger Troy.

Supporting line (About page):
> Founded in 1986 by congregations across the Troy area, TAUM welcomes people of every faith and no faith at all.

## Option B — leads with the building
> At 392 Second Street, TAUM opens its doors to the whole community — with free meals, groceries, and a Free Food Fridge; furniture for families making a fresh start; summer jobs and tech skills for teens; and care for anyone facing illness, homelessness, or hard times. Rooted in Troy's interfaith community, open to all.

## Option C — traditional mission format
> TAUM works alongside our neighbors in the Troy area to meet essential needs — food, furniture, and a place to belong. We support people navigating illness, homelessness, and domestic violence; prepare young people for work and life; offer chaplaincy and a listening ear; and open our building as a gathering place for the whole community.
