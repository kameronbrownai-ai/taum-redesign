MLK Scholarship

Investing in the dream, one student at a time.

Since 1997, TAUM's Rev. Martin Luther King Jr. Scholarship has helped Troy-area graduates take the next step to college. Renamed in 2015 to honor Ronald J. Dukes, longtime MLK Committee Chairman, the scholarship supports students committed to Dr. King's vision — young people ready to confront racism, poverty, and injustice in their own communities.

Who can apply

  • High school seniors or GED recipients entering college
  • Rensselaer County residents, or members of a TAUM congregation
  • (Immediate family of TAUM staff and board aren't eligible)

What we look for

Commitment to Dr. King's goals · financial need · academic record · community service · personal goals · a finalist interview

How to apply

  1. Download the application — 2026 MLK Scholarship Application (PDF)
  2. Gather your materials — your essay, transcripts, and a recommendation from a school official or community leader
  3. Mail it postmarked by May 4 to: MLK Scholarship Committee, 392 Second St., Troy, NY 12180

For students who show up for their community

This scholarship isn't only about grades. It's for young people already doing the work — mentoring, organizing, serving — who carry Dr. King's vision into their own neighborhoods. We help with the next step.

Congratulations to our 2025 scholars

Seven students, now at SUNY Oneonta, HVCC, University at Buffalo, and beyond.
