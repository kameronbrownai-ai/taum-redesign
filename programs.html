Programs

What we do, and who to ask.

TAUM runs six programs out of 392 Second Street, each with its own team. Find the one you need below, then reach the person who runs it directly. Not sure where to start? Call the office at (518) 274-5920 and we'll point you the right way.

Food

Free hot lunches on weekdays, groceries during office hours, and a Free Food Fridge that's open 24/7. No ID, no referral, no questions asked.

Who to contact

Barbara Healey, Damien Center Coordinator
(518) 274-5920 ext. 206

Learn more about Food →

The Furniture Program

Good, used furniture, free of charge, with free delivery, for families leaving shelters, domestic violence survivors, fire victims, seniors, and neighbors starting over. We also pick up donations.

Who to contact

Tyara Burnett, Program Director
(518) 274-5920 ext. 204

Learn more about Furniture →

Tech for Teens

A free summer program for Rensselaer County teens, paid work experience plus training in Office, web design, and coding. Every graduate keeps a refurbished laptop.

Who to contact

Program office
(518) 274-5920 ext. 202

Learn more about Tech for Teens →

The Damien Center

A hospitality center for neighbors living with serious illness, meals, friendship, peer support, and help finding services. Everyone is welcome at the table.

Who to contact

Barbara Healey, Coordinator
(518) 274-5920 ext. 206

Learn more about the Damien Center →

Campus Chaplaincy

Pastoral care, de-stress meditation, and care packages for students and staff at Russell Sage College, every background, every belief, or none. Care, never conversion.

Who to contact

Darren Gundrum, Chaplain
518-244-4522 · gundrd@sage.edu

Learn more about Chaplaincy →

MLK Scholarship

College scholarships for Rensselaer County graduates committed to Dr. King's vision, awarded every year since 1997. Applications are due, postmarked, by May 4.

Who to contact

Rev. Abby Norton-Levering, Executive Director
nortonlevering@taum.org

Learn more about the Scholarship →

One more: the CROP Hunger Walk

Every spring, the first Sunday in May, TAUM hosts the Troy/Rensselaer CROP Hunger Walk, raising money for hunger relief here and around the world. It's a program and an event rolled into one. Find it on our Get Involved page.

Not sure which door to knock on?

That's what the front desk is for. Call (518) 274-5920 during office hours (Monday to Friday, 9 am to 4 pm) and Lynn will connect you with the right program and person, or just send us a message.
