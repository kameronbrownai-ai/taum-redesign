Community Center

The living room of the neighborhood.

392 Second Street is not just an office with a food pantry in it. It's where neighbors eat together, where groups meet, where classes run, where people organize for a fairer Troy. If you need a room, a kitchen, or just a place to be, this building is yours.

Ask about using the space See what's on

What's in the building

🪑 The community room

Our largest gathering space, set up for meetings, classes, workshops, and celebrations. Tables and chairs included.

🍳 The kitchen

Where Monday dinner and Thursday lunch come from. Available to groups cooking for the neighborhood.

🗂️ Meeting space

Smaller rooms for a committee, a support group, a tutoring session, or a quiet conversation.

🧺 Food programs

The pantry, the Thursday grocery distribution, and the Free Food Fridge out front all live here. See times.

💛 The Damien Center

Our hospitality program for neighbors living with serious illness meets in this building too. Learn more.

📣 Room to organize

Space for neighbors working toward justice, healing, and peace in Troy, whether that's a coalition meeting or a know-your-rights night.

Open to every kind of neighbor

Faith groups and secular groups. Block associations and book clubs. Twelve-step meetings, tenant meetings, craft nights, funerals, baby showers, and budget hearings. We were founded by congregations, but you don't have to believe anything in particular to walk through the door and use this place.

If your group serves the neighborhood and money is tight, say so. We will work it out.

Partners who work out of here

We share our building with organizations doing work that lines up with ours, so neighbors can find more than one kind of help in one stop.

Community Compass

A partner program helping neighbors find their way to the services they need, from benefits and housing to health care and everything in between. Ask at the office and we'll point you to them.

To reach them: call TAUM at (518) 274-5920 and we'll connect you.

Need a room?

Tell us what you're planning, roughly how many people, and when. Lynn will walk you through what's available.
