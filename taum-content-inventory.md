## 3. Programs (the core content)

### Troy Damien Center
- Hospitality center for persons affected by HIV/AIDS and other serious illnesses.
- Services: **meals (also open to anyone experiencing food insecurity), social & recreational opportunities, peer support, education, referral.**
- Located at 392 Second Street.
- **Contact:** Barbara Healey, Damien Center Coordinator — (518) 274-5920 x206
- Image on old site: `Damien_slider.20180206`
- ⚠️ Gap: no hours, no meal schedule, no photos of the space. Abby wants "lots more information about our programs."

### The Furniture Program
- Good-condition used furniture, **free of charge**, to families in need: women & children fleeing domestic violence, families leaving homeless shelters, working poor, persons with disabilities, elderly poor, people affected by fire/catastrophe.
- **Accepted:** beds, dressers, tables, chairs, living room furniture.
- **Not accepted:** sleeper sofas, damaged furniture needing repair.
- **Pickup offered** for donated furniture.
- **Contact:** Tyara Burnett, Program Director — (518) 274-5920 x204
- Monetary contributions requested to sustain operations.
- Image: `taum-truck.jpg`
- Abby: "How to donate furniture" is one of the most-used pages → deserves a prominent, simple page/flow.
- ⚠️ Gap: no referral process detail for recipients, no service area, no hours.

### Tech for Teens
- Intensive **summer program** (also described as afterschool on /programs/) teaching essential skills + providing employment for low-income and at-risk youth in Rensselaer County.
- Curriculum: Microsoft Office Suite, resume writing, Excel, PowerPoint, web design, coding.
- **Students receive a refurbished PC laptop.**
- Employment through **Rensselaer County Summer Youth Employment Program** (https://www.rensco.com/406/Youth).
- Contact: ext. 202 (Abby / Exec Director's line).
- Application info posted "when the application period opens."
- Image: `Slider-bg-Tech-for-Teens.jpg`

### Campus Ministry at Russell Sage College
- Serves students and staff of all backgrounds: pastoral care, de-stress meditation, care packages, spiritual formation. Collaborates with campus wellness/academic departments.
- **Contact:** Darren Gundrum, Protestant Chaplain — 518-244-4522, gundrd@sage.edu
- Image: `Gundrum.150x150x72.png`

### MLK Scholarships and Events
- Honors Dr. King via January holiday activities, race-relations initiatives, and college scholarships for Troy-area high school graduates.
- **Scholarship established 1997; renamed 2015 for Ronald J. Dukes**, former MLK Committee Chairman.
- Themes: exploring "racism, sexism, classism, militarism, homophobia, ageism, and prejudice."
- **Award criteria (6):** commitment to Dr. King's goals, financial need, academic record, community service, personal goals, finalist interview.
- **Eligibility (3):** HS senior/GED recipient entering college; Rensselaer County resident or member of a TAUM congregation; TAUM staff/board family excluded.
- **Application:** PDF form; postmark deadline May 4, 2026 (annual); mail to MLK Scholarship Committee, 392 Second St., Troy NY 12180.
- **Contact:** Abby Norton-Levering — nortonlevering@taum.org
- 2025: seven recipients (SUNY Oneonta, HVCC, University at Buffalo, etc.)
- Contact ext. 201. Image: `Slider-bg-King.jpg`

### CROP Hunger Walk (listed as program AND event)
- Troy/Rensselaer CROP Hunger Walk — annual, first Sunday in May (next: **Sunday May 3, 2026, 1 PM**, starts at TAUM).
- Routes: 3-mile traditional + kid-friendly "Golden Mile."
- Funds global hunger relief + local Rensselaer County agencies.
- Register/fundraise: https://www.crophungerwalk.org/troyny/
- Image: `HungerWalk.jpg`
