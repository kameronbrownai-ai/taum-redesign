Tech for Teens

Real skills. Real paychecks. Real laptops.

Tech for Teens is an intensive summer program for Rensselaer County youth. Teens earn a paycheck through the Rensselaer County Summer Youth Employment Program while building skills that last: Microsoft Office, Excel, PowerPoint, resume writing, web design, and coding. Every student who completes the summer takes home a refurbished laptop of their own.

How it works

When
Six weeks each summer, July to August, weekday mornings
Where
TAUM, 392 Second Street, Troy
Who
Rensselaer County teens, ages 14 to 18
Cost
Free, and you get paid through the county's Summer Youth Employment Program
Bonus
Keep the refurbished laptop you train on

Applications open each spring

Sign up below and we'll email you the moment applications open. No need to keep checking back.

Notify me when applications open

Employment applications go through the Rensselaer County Summer Youth Employment Program.
